Welcome to SeacoastDJ

Music that's actually built for your crowd.

I bought my first pair of turntables in 1999, and by 2004 I'd turned that into my first paid solo gig. I founded Seacoast DJ & Marketing Services, LLC in 2016 to make it my full-time craft — weddings, private parties, and corporate events across the New Hampshire and Massachusetts Seacoast. I don't work off a fixed playlist: every event starts with a real conversation about your crowd, your must-plays, and the moments that matter most, then I build and run the set live.
