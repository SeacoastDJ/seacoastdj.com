School dance FAQ

Answers for advisors and student councils.

Do you play clean music at school dances?

Yes. I play radio or clean versions of any song with explicit lyrics, so you can trust what comes through the speakers in front of students and chaperones.

How do students request songs?

Each dance gets its own online song request link. Your student council or class officers share it ahead of time, and I clear every request before the night. Because every song is checked first, on-the-spot requests are limited, so get them in early.

Are you insured? Our district needs a certificate.

Yes. Seacoast DJ is licensed and insured, and I send a certificate of insurance (COI) straight to your school or venue.

Who do you work with to plan the dance?

Whoever is running it: a class advisor, the student council, an activities director, or a parent group. One planning call covers the timeline, announcements, and any royalty court or senior moments.

Do you cover graduation celebrations and banquets too?

Yes. Senior banquets, graduation celebrations, and 8th grade dances get the same clean music, MC support, and sound.
