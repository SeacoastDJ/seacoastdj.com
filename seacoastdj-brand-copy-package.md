## 1. Brand Position (one paragraph, keep this pinned somewhere)

Seacoast DJ is New England's high-energy, personally-run alternative to a faceless "wedding DJ company." Bill Pyndo has been DJing live (real mixing, not just a playlist on shuffle) since 2004, and runs Seacoast DJ & Marketing Services, LLC out of Hampton, NH. The brand promise: every client works directly with Bill, gets a set built around *their* crowd — with real strength serving the 20–40 age range that actually fills a dance floor — and gets professional-grade sound, MC, lighting, and video, not a stripped-down basic package.

**Say it the same way everywhere:** "Seacoast DJ, led by DJ Bill Pyndo" — not sometimes "SeacoastDJ," sometimes "Seacoast DJ & Marketing Services, LLC," sometimes just "DJ Bill Pyndo." Pick one hierarchy: **Seacoast DJ** is the public-facing brand name everywhere (site, socials, GBP, GigBuilder). **Seacoast DJ & Marketing Services, LLC** only appears where a legal entity name is required (contracts, invoices, insurance certs). **DJ Bill Pyndo** is the "led by" personal credibility line, not a competing brand name.

**Milestones — state both, every time you mention history, so it doesn't look like conflicting facts:**
"DJing since 2004 · Seacoast DJ & Marketing Services, LLC established 2016."
